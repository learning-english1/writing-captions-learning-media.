Activity 3 – Listening

Understanding Captions for Photographs, Tables, and Graphs

Instructions

Listen to the short audio in which a speaker introduces three visuals: a photograph, a table, and a graph. Complete the notes while listening. You may listen more than once.

Listening focus: Identify what each visual shows, the key details (who, where, when, or numbers), and the social function of each visual.

Listening Audio

Press Play to listen to the speaker. You can pause or replay the audio as needed.

If your browser does not support audio playback, ask your teacher to read the listening script aloud.

Teacher's script (open after listening)

Good morning, everyone. Today I will introduce three visuals about digital life at school. First, the photograph shows students in the computer laboratory at Merdeka Senior High School on Monday, 9 November 2026. They are learning how to create a class presentation using computers. This photograph helps the audience see what digital learning looks like in our school. Second, the table shows the number of students who used the school learning platform during one week. On Monday, 40 students logged in; on Tuesday, 50 students; and on Wednesday, 65 students. The table helps readers compare the number of users on each day. The data was collected by the school's ICT team. Third, the line graph shows the number of students who submitted their English assignments online over four weeks. The number increased from 20 students in Week 1 to 35 in Week 2, 45 in Week 3, and 60 in Week 4. The graph makes the upward trend easy to see. The table and graph use data collected by the school's ICT team in November 2026. Thank you.

Comprehension Questions

Remember

  • A caption explains the information shown in a visual.
  • Use accurate details, including dates and numbers.
  • Give credit to the source of information or visuals when necessary.
  • Choose a caption that helps the audience understand the visual clearly.
